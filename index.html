What PSG is

I founded PSG, a self-funded startup, in June 2026, after 16 years as a product manager in healthcare practice-management software. PSG is a multi-discipline practice-management and health-records platform for small health and wellness practices. One shared core handles scheduling, billing, patient messaging, records and compliance, and each discipline (chiropractic first) plugs in its own charts and workflows.

PSG is also an AI product lab: a deliberate test of how far a Principal PM and a junior engineer can take enterprise-grade, regulated software when AI does most of the writing.

Where to start

  • Voice Session Compliance Review Open → AI-assisted charting with guardrails. Voice-captured content stays a draft until the provider reviews it, recording consent is checked first, and raw audio is reachable only through this audited review screen.
  • Schedule Open → Where every role lands after login: provider and room views with appointment detail, alongside the Action Center task pane.
  • Patient Flow Open → A check-in to check-out workbench. Every visit follows one generic three-stage shape, and each discipline defines the actions inside each stage.
  • Operations Dashboard Open → Work in flight, banded Watch, Overdue and Critical. It exists because the Action Center was redesigned as a checklist that clears when you have done your part, not a dashboard of unfinished work.
  • Unified Inbox Open → Patient conversations across text, email and the patient portal in one place, organized by queues and tags, with snooze, auto-reopen and internal notes.
