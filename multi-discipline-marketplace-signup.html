3a
How will your disciplines share patients?

Asked only when two or more disciplines were selected (Chiropractic and Physical Therapy here), right after the practice is created and before Account setup. Required: there is no default, so Continue stays disabled until one option is chosen. The answer sets the practice-wide default; it can be changed later in Clinical View Scope Settings.

Will providers across the locations you add coordinate care on shared patients, or should each location’s activity stay separate by default?

CoordinatedProviders see a shared patient’s full clinical history across disciplines by default.
Clinic-scopedEach location sees only its own clinic’s activity by default. Anyone can still expand to the full record.

Coordinated shows every discipline’s chart to every Location you add.

Every provider and staff member across every Location you add will see a shared patient’s full history from every other discipline by default. Only choose Coordinated if every Location is genuinely coordinating care on shared patients — not just sharing one patient record.

Either way, nobody’s access changes — this only sets what opens by default. The warning stays visible so it’s read before choosing Coordinated.

3b
Settings your disciplines suggest differently

Asked right after the question above, only when two or more disciplines were selected and their suggestions differ for at least one practice-wide setting (here: Health history review and Move to Reengage after). Owner only. Confirm settings saves any change and records the confirmation; Decide later saves nothing and puts “Confirm settings your disciplines suggest differently” on the setup checklist; it reopens from the wizard’s readiness item. Then the Owner’s set-password email.
