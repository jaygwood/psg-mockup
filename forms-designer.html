Reference — Photography consent field selected int-16 · Add field › Photography consent · 2026-10-29, D4
Consent wording
{practice name} would like your permission to take photos as part of your care. Check each use you agree to. You can say no to any of them, and your care does not depend on your answer.

Each checked box records that use on the patient’s photography consent. No Required switch: every use is the patient’s choice. A form can hold one Photography consent field; the option is greyed out once added. Only on Photography consent forms: on any other form it is greyed out.

Marketing and publication adds the authorization, signed on its own signature line: what the photos show and how they will be used, who may use them, who may receive them, when permission ends, the right to cancel in writing and how, that treatment does not depend on signing, and that the people who receive the photos may pass them on and federal privacy law may then no longer protect them.
Expires after

What the patient sees





Permission for marketing and publication ends 2 years after signing unless it is cancelled sooner.

Sign to allow marketing and publication — its own signature line, separate from the form’s signature. Leave it unsigned and the other choices still stand. You will be given a copy of what you sign.
Reference — field row menu Duplicate and Remove

Each field row’s menu: Edit options (choice fields), Add condition…, Duplicate and Remove. Duplicate places a copy right below with “(copy)” on its label. Remove acts at once — the draft isn’t saved until Save draft, and a toast offers Undo:

Removed “Reason for Visit”.

Removing a field another field’s condition depends on asks first: “Remove ‘How did you hear about us?’? The rule on ‘Who referred you?’ is removed too.”
