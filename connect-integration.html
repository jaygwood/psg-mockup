Workflow · CONNECT-INTEGRATION-SCREEN · Present (full) · HL7 / FHIR MPI · currently Error

Entry points on Integration Connections: Connect on a category with no connection, and Reconnect on a connection that is in error, disabled or needs its settings changed. Org Admin, Owner and IT Admin (ADR-471, Phase 3–4) only — hidden, not shown-disabled, for Office Admin (ADR-452) and every role without connect authority (AC-09). A system process may also connect during migration setup (ADR-089 Decision 6), with no UI of its own.

2026-10-15 (ADR-716): this credential flow is for practice-held categories (HL7 / FHIR MPI at Phase 1). Payment Gateway and Clearinghouse are PSG-held — their Connect is Request activation with no credential or ID fields, and they have no Reconnect. Before either flow saves anything, PSG’s vendor-agreement check runs; if it fails: “This service isn’t available yet — contact PSG support.”

1
Establish credential

Category (HL7 / FHIR MPI) and vendor (AWS HealthLake, a realistic placeholder — PSG hasn’t chosen this vendor) come from the row you reconnected; there is no vendor picker at Phase 1 (one vendor per category). Direct credential entry shown (fields are vendor-defined). Any webhook endpoint the vendor needs registers in this same step (Workflow 6, AC-15).

The stored credential is never shown. Reconnecting always issues a fresh one; from a disabled row, the old secret is already scheduled for deletion.

2
Submit and resolve

On submit, the platform validates the credential. Success: a new Secrets Manager secret is created, the connection’s stored secret reference is updated on the same row, its status becomes Connected, who connected it, when, and when the credential was rotated are recorded, any disabled details are cleared, and the event is written to platform audit log. The Org Admin health-check task clears. Failure: nothing is persisted, the row keeps its status, inline error shown, back to step 1.
