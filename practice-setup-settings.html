Reference — Turning a module off Off, before it saves

Choosing Off in either column asks first; On and Discipline default save at once. The consequence line names what Off does for that module: Workers’ Comp and Inventory disappear from navigation at that location, and Inventory’s Supplies used section leaves charts there (records and logged supplies are kept); Memberships and the billing arrangements stop new sign-ups only, and existing ones carry on.

Turn off Employer billing at Downtown Office?

No new employer invoices are created for Downtown Office visits. Open invoices can still be finished and collected, and employer contacts keep their history.

Reference — Turning the patient portal off confirm

Office Admin can change the portal switches for their own location; Org Admin and Owner for any location or the whole practice. The two sub-settings show only while the portal is on and save at once.

Turn off the patient portal at Downtown Office?

Patients at Downtown Office can’t sign in to the portal, and new portal invitations stop. Their accounts, messages and forms are kept and come back when it’s turned on again.
