Reference — Clinical View Scope step Before Account Setup · sales-led or resumed Owner

Shown the first time the Owner signs in to a practice with two or more disciplines whose Clinical View Scope is unanswered (sales-led creation, or a self-serve signup left before the question). Same question and Coordinated warning as Multi-Discipline Marketplace Signup Step 3a.

Will providers across the locations you add coordinate care on shared patients, or should each location’s activity stay separate by default?

CoordinatedProviders see a shared patient’s full clinical history across disciplines by default.
Clinic-scopedEach location sees only its own clinic’s activity by default. Anyone can still expand to the full record.

Coordinated shows every discipline’s chart to every Location you add.

Every provider and staff member across every Location you add will see a shared patient’s full history from every other discipline by default. Only choose Coordinated if every Location is genuinely coordinating care on shared patients — not just sharing one patient record.

Either way, nobody’s access changes — this only sets what opens by default. The warning stays visible so it’s read before choosing Coordinated.

Reference — Learn more popover Opens from the clinic help line · in the app
Setting up your clinics
  • Add one location for each clinic — the rooms, providers and staff who book and check patients in together.
  • Two clinics in one building are two locations. Each has its own check-in and check-out, cash drawer and supplies.
  • Staff who cover several clinics get a role at each and switch between them.
  • Each clinic belongs to one billing entity. A clinic of a second entity needs its own fee schedule.
  • Banner panels follow each clinic’s discipline. To show a panel at every clinic, set it practice-wide in Patient Banner settings.

Learn more on the clinic help line (int-04) opens this small popover instead of a Help & Support article. It is help only: it adds no field and saves nothing. Same popover on Practice Signup & Onboarding Wizard Step 2 and on Locations’ Add location.
