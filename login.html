States · Sign-in messages (inline, never a toast)
PSG

Below the password field

Incorrect email or password.

Wrong password (fewer than 5 tries in a row), unknown email, or a declined watch request — the same wording for all three.

Too many attempts. Try again in 15 minutes or ask your Office Admin to unlock your account.

5th failure in a row, and every attempt while the account is still locked — even with the right password.

We couldn’t find your account. Ask your practice’s Office Admin or Owner to invite you first.

Single sign-on succeeded at the identity provider but no PSG user has that email. PSG never creates the account automatically.

This practice’s account is offline. Its data was exported and archived — nothing was deleted. See account information →

Right email and password (or SSO, watch or MFA approval), but the practice’s account has gone offline: no session starts and the link opens the Account-Offline page. Wrong credentials still get “Incorrect email or password”. A practice that is closing signs in as usual and sees the app read-only. The same notice shows above the email field when a session ends because the practice went offline.
