When the trigger needs a number

Appointment upcoming and Manual enrollment need none (with Manual enrollment, staff add patients from the Patient Record’s Campaigns panel). Each trigger below shows its one number field right under Trigger; Membership renewal approaching takes its timing from the membership plan instead:

Push-channel advisory (AC-08), shown for reference

Reference — same-day reminder with Ask the patient to confirm 2026-10-07 · O1

A patient with several visits on one day gets one reminder per channel listing them all, whichever clinics they’re at (comm-design.md Workflow 18). With Ask the patient to confirm on, the fixed line is added once. “C” confirms every listed visit still unconfirmed; the link opens a page where each visit is confirmed on its own (conversations.md Workflow 41). A visit sent to a confidential-care contact is never combined with the others.

Reference — Activate dialog (int-01) informed confirmation

Activate… saves unsaved edits first. The dialog tells you who a marketing step will reach; it doesn’t stop you activating. Consent is checked again for each patient every time a step sends.
